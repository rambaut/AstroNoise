Model. Per-pixel rates assume a flat spectrum with a V-band zero point of 10⁸ photons s⁻¹ m⁻² nm⁻¹. Sub noise is √(target + sky + dark + read²), with quantisation noise (e⁻/ADU ÷ √12) folded into read noise; stacking N subs improves SNR by √N. Dynamic range is log₂ of the headroom left above the background divided by the background noise. Calibration frames are assumed noiseless.

Camera data. Preset values are approximate transcriptions of published ZWO specifications and gain curves. Check them against the data sheet for your camera; every value can be overridden in the Camera panel.
